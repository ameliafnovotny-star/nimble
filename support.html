Frequently Asked Questions

Is an account required?

No. You can use Nimble entirely without an account. All your workouts are saved locally on your device. Creating an account is only needed if you want to back up your data to the cloud and sync across devices.

How do I back up my data?

Go to the Account tab and create a free account. Once signed in, your data syncs automatically every few seconds.

I lost my data — can I recover it?

If you were signed in, your data is backed up to the cloud. Sign back in on the Account tab and tap Sync Now to restore it. If you were not signed in, data is stored only on your device and cannot be recovered after uninstalling the app.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at amelia.f.novotny@gmail.com with your account email and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data.
