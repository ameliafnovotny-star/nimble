Privacy Policy for FitTrack

Last updated: May 15, 2026

FitTrack ("the App") is a personal fitness tracking application. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data We Collect

FitTrack does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter — workouts, exercises, weights, reps, and session history — is stored only on your device using local storage.

Data Storage

  • All app data is stored locally on your device.
  • No data is sent to any server or third party.
  • No account or login is required.
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all stored data.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
